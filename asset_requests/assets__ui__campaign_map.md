# Campaign landscape

[Production artwork](../assets/ui/campaign_map.png) · 356 × 336 px · Opaque RGB

Godot samples the landscape across the 40 interactive counties. The terrain starts at screen y=24 under the campaign header. Ownership colors, borders, selection highlights, HQ buildings and flags are drawn at runtime. The [atlas sheet](../assets/ui/campaign_atlas.png) supplies the printed map surround and the Gulf.

## Built-in image generation prompt

Square pixel-art overworld terrain background for a 1930s Texas oil tycoon game, a jewel-like original 16-bit map viewed from high overhead at a slight three-quarter angle. Fill entire square edge to edge with land. Bottom third ochre grassland, terraced farm fields, scattered mesquite. Middle bands transition from eastern pine woods to red rock mesas and sage scrub. Upper third a winding narrow turquoise bayou entering from upper right, refinery tanks far up north and distant city buildings at the very top edge. Charming hand-placed pixel clusters, warm parchment tan, olive greens, dusty coral stone, deep teal water, afternoon shadows. Broad quiet open clearings throughout for placing twenty tiny village sprites and winding roads at runtime. Landscape is the subject. No text, numbers, borders, labels, map pins, roads, route markings, or interface. Purpose-built low-resolution terrain reminiscent of vintage strategy game world maps, readable at 356x336 pixels.
